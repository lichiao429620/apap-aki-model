How to use

Enter the first laboratory values obtained within 24 h of admission. Fields left blank are treated as missing; the model handles missing values natively and does not require imputation. The calculator returns the predicted probability of AKI and a risk band.

Disclaimer

This tool is for research and educational use only. It is not a medical device and must not be the sole basis for clinical decisions. The model has not been validated prospectively and predicted probabilities should be recalibrated on local data before any clinical use.

All calculations run locally in your browser. No patient data is transmitted to any server.
